# Publishing your place

Aero keeps a copy of your place on your account, so the file on your computer is never the only one.

Open your place in Studio and choose File > Publish to Aero. Give it a name and a description, then press Publish. Studio uploads the place as it is at that moment. The cover starts as a frame of your current view, so frame a shot you like before you publish. Once you choose your own image in a publish window, Studio keeps it through later publishes, and the Capture viewport button in that window frames a new shot for you. Choose who can play the place before you publish as well. Private keeps it to you, and that is how new places start, after which Studio remembers the visibility you last published with. Friends shares it with your friends, unlisted is for anyone with the link, and public lists it on the Games hub. You can change this each time you publish.

Two optional fields ride along with a publish. Genres (up to three, from a fixed list) tell players what kind of game it is; they show on the game page and its tiles, and you can change them any time in the publish window or on the website. What's new is a short release note for that version. Fill it in or leave it blank; a version never needs one. Notes you do write appear on the game page under "What's new", and you can add or edit them later on the website's Versions tab for that place.

Players who visit your game's page can like or dislike it. The percent of players who liked it shows on the page and on tiles around the site. Votes are per account, you cannot see who voted, and players can change or remove their vote.

Publishing again is an update. Each publish becomes a new version, and the newest one is what people see. Aero Places (File > Aero Places) lists everything on your account with its latest version. Open downloads a local copy and opens it, and Save As... writes one wherever you want. Only you can download your places.

The Create page on the website is your management view. The Places tab lists everything you have published with its visibility, live player count, and visits. The Insights tab shows how your places are doing: visits over the last 30 days, total visits, unique players, which place is being played now, and a reach card with how often your places appeared on the Games page or home recommendations, how many of those looks became visits, and how many people chose not to see a place again. Reach counts start when the feature launched, so charts fill in over the first weeks.

If Aero staff delist a place for breaking the [community guidelines](https://aeroengine.org/guidelines), it turns private, its servers stop, and you get the reason by email, on the place's page, and on your [Safety](https://aeroengine.org/safety) page. The visibility choices lock to private until staff relist the place. When you have fixed the problem, open the place's page and use Request review.
