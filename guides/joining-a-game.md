# Joining a game

Games on the Games hub run on Aero's servers. You do not need to host anything yourself.

Open a game's page and press Join. The platform puts you on a server with room, or starts one for that place, and opens the Aero Client on your computer. The first join takes a moment while the server starts.

The Servers section lists the servers running for that game. Press Join on a server to pick it, or press Join at the top of the page to let the platform choose. A server stops on its own about two minutes after the last player leaves.

The actions menu (the three dots next to Join) copies the place's ID or a link to the page, and reports the place when something breaks the [community guidelines](https://aeroengine.org/guidelines).

If nothing opens when you press Join, the Aero Client is not installed. Install Aero from the downloads page and press Join again.
